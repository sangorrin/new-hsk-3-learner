# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
delegated: static React + TypeScript + Vite, deployed to GitHub Pages

## Users
Spanish- and English-speaking learners studying the New HSK 3 syllabus by lesson on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose
New-HSK-3-Learner turns the provided lesson workbooks into a focused practice tool for hanzi, vocabulary, textbook phrases, grammar, and flashcards. Success means a learner can choose a lesson, study connected content, hear pronunciation, and retain progress locally.

## Positioning
A lesson-first study surface that keeps vocabulary, phrases, grammar, and character practice connected to the same workbook source.

## Operating Context
Self-directed study in a browser, often in short sessions. Data is static and progress is stored in the learner's browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Five modes: Hanzi, Words, Phrases, Grammar, Flashcards. Search, lesson filtering, speech synthesis, favorites, local progress, cross-mode links, and GitHub Pages compatibility are required. English workbook translations are authoritative; Spanish UI labels supplement them without fabricated translations.

## Brand Commitments
Product name: New-HSK-3-Learner. Bilingual English + Spanish presentation.

## Evidence on Hand
`REFERENCES/hsk3-vocabulary-complete-lessons-1-18.xlsx`; `REFERENCES/hsk3-grammar-complete-lessons-1-18.xlsx`; `REFERENCES/MNN-N5-Kanji` behavioral reference scaffold.

## Product Principles
- Keep every study action close to its lesson context.
- Make source truth visible and traceable.
- Reward short, repeatable practice sessions.
- Keep the interface calm, legible, and tactile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive layout, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, and bilingual labels are required.
